## Data source note

The dataset was collected from a social media platform and is redistributed in
preprocessed, de-identified form (no raw tweet text and no account
identifiers; user mentions replaced with `@USER`, URLs with `HTTPURL`) in
accordance with the platform's Terms of Service. The CC BY 4.0 license applies
